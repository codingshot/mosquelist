# MosqueList — AI & Search Indexing

MosqueList (https://mosquelist.com) is a travel and discovery site for the world's most significant and beautiful mosques. It helps users explore, plan, and track spiritual journeys and build a personal "bucket list" of mosques to visit.

## What this site offers
- **Explore**: Searchable catalog of mosques with details (location, capacity, history, architecture, visitor info).
- **Curated lists**: Holy Sites (Mecca, Medina, Jerusalem), largest mosques, by country (Turkey, UAE, Malaysia, etc.).
- **Map**: Interactive map of mosque locations.
- **Timeline**: Key dates in mosque history and construction.
- **My List (bucket list)**: Users can add mosques to a personal list and reorder; stored in browser.
- **Guides**: Travel and visitor tips for mosque visits.

## Key pages
- Home: https://mosquelist.com
- Explore (all mosques): https://mosquelist.com/explore
- Map: https://mosquelist.com/map
- Lists: https://mosquelist.com/lists
- Timeline: https://mosquelist.com/timeline
- Individual mosque pages: https://mosquelist.com/mosque/{id}
- List detail pages: https://mosquelist.com/lists/{slug}

## Structured data
- Homepage: WebSite schema with SearchAction.
- Mosque pages: Place schema (name, address, description, image, amenities).
- List pages: ItemList schema with ListItem entries linking to Place pages.

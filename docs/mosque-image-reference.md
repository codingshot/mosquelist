## How to Convert Special:FilePath URLs

To convert a Special:FilePath URL to a direct upload URL:

1. Go to `https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:FILENAME`
2. Click "Original file" link
3. Copy the direct URL (format: `https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/X/XX/Filename.jpg`)

Example:
- **Before**: `https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Jamia_Masjid_Srinagar.jpg`
- **After**: `https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/ab/Jamia_Masjid_Srinagar.jpg`

## Image Quality Notes

- Prefer images with resolution 1024×768 or higher
- JPEG format is preferred for photos
- Ensure images show the mosque exterior clearly
- Include interior/night shots in gallery when available

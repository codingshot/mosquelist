## One-sentence product description (pick by audience)

- **General:** MosqueList helps you discover, explore, and plan visits to the world’s most significant mosques—with a personal bucket list and map.
- **Muslims:** One place to discover sacred and significant mosques, save favorites, and track your spiritual journey.
- **Travelers:** Find mosques by country, see which are open to visitors, and plan your next trip—with practical tips.
- **Press/partners:** MosqueList is a free web app that helps people discover and plan visits to the world’s most significant mosques—from the three holiest sites to architectural landmarks in 40+ countries—with filters, a map, and a personal mosque bucket list.

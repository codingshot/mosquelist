# Channel Playbook — MosqueList

Per-channel strategy: goals, content mix, and tactics. Align with [customer-personas.md](./customer-personas.md) and [content-types.md](./content-types.md). **Internal only.**

---

## Instagram

**Goals:** Discovery, aspiration, bucket-list saves; reach Muslims and cultural travelers.

**Content mix:**
- 40% inspirational (mosque images, “places to pray,” bucket list)
- 30% educational (one mosque fact, “biggest in X,” visitor tip)
- 20% product (how to use MosqueList, map, lists)
- 10% seasonal (Ramadan, Eid, Hajj prep)

**Tactics:**
- Carousels for “5 mosques in [country]” or “Timeline: 622 CE to today”
- Reels: 15–30 sec “How I use MosqueList” or “3 mosques to add to your list”
- Stories: poll (“Which region next?”), “Swipe to explore,” link sticker to site
- Hashtags: 5–8 per post; mix #MosqueList #IslamicHeritage #MuslimTravel #BucketList #SacredPlaces
- Bio: One-line pitch + link to site (or linktree if multiple links)

**Posting:** 3–5x/week; at least 1 Reel/week if resources allow.

---

## Twitter / X

**Goals:** Product updates, travel/Islamic community conversation, SEO and backlinks.

**Content mix:**
- Threads: “We built MosqueList because…”, “How to plan a mosque-focused trip”
- Single tweets: one mosque fact, “New: timeline by region,” link to explore
- Replies: Engage with travel, Islamic heritage, bucket-list conversations (no spam)

**Tactics:**
- Thread opener with clear value; 5–7 tweets; CTA to site in last tweet
- Use “mosques,” “Islamic heritage,” “bucket list,” “Masjid” in copy for discoverability
- Quote-tweet travel or Islamic content with a short add (“You can explore and save these on MosqueList”)

**Posting:** 5–7x/week; 1–2 threads per month.

---

## TikTok

**Goals:** Viral discovery among younger Muslims and travel enthusiasts; “mosque bucket list” trend.

**Content mix:**
- “Where do you want to pray next?” — 15s hook + feature overview
- “POV: You’re building your mosque bucket list”
- “Biggest mosque in [country]” with one fact + CTA
- Duet/Stitch with travel or Islamic content (respectful)

**Tactics:**
- Hook in first 2 seconds; text overlay for key points; CTA “Link in bio”
- Sound: trending when it fits; otherwise calm, respectful music
- Hashtags: #MosqueList #IslamicTravel #BucketList #Mosques #Travel

**Posting:** 2–4x/week if capacity allows.

---

## Facebook

**Goals:** Older Muslims, community groups, family sharing; longer-form and link sharing.

**Content mix:**
- Same as Instagram but longer captions; link to site in post
- “We added 10 new mosques — explore by country and region”
- Share blog/guide links (visitor tips, travel guide)

**Tactics:**
- Post in relevant groups only when allowed (no spam); provide value first
- Use “Save” and “Share” prompts in copy
- Consider lightweight paid boost for Ramadan or launch (audience: Muslims, travel, interests in Islamic culture)

**Posting:** 2–3x/week.

---

## LinkedIn

**Goals:** Credibility, press, partners, “project we built” narrative.

**Content mix:**
- Launch and feature announcements (professional tone)
- “How we’re building a respectful discovery tool for significant mosques”
- No memes; minimal hashtags; focus on product and impact

**Tactics:**
- Post as founder/team; invite feedback from travel and tech network
- Connect with travel tech, Islamic education, and impact-focused folks; don’t hard-sell
- Article or long post for “Why we built MosqueList” (once)

**Posting:** 1–2x/month or on major releases.

---

## Pinterest

**Goals:** Evergreen traffic; “mosques to visit,” “Islamic architecture,” “bucket list” searches.

**Content mix:**
- Pins for each major mosque (image + title + description with keywords)
- Boards: “Holy Sites,” “Mosques by Country,” “Visitor-Friendly Mosques,” “Islamic Architecture”
- Infographics: “Largest mosques in the world,” “Three holiest sites”

**Tactics:**
- Vertical images (1000x1500 or similar); text overlay with keyword-rich title
- Description: 1–2 sentences + link to site; use “mosque,” “visit,” “bucket list,” country names
- Link every pin to relevant MosqueList page (explore, mosque page, or list)

**Posting:** 5–10 pins/week; repin and organize boards regularly.

---

## YouTube

**Goals:** SEO, “how to plan,” “biggest mosques,” visitor guides; long-term asset.

**Content mix:**
- Shorts: 60–90 sec “5 mosques to add to your bucket list,” “How to use MosqueList”
- Longer (5–10 min): “Planning a mosque-focused trip,” “Visitor tips for Sheikh Zayed / Blue Mosque”
- Playlists: By region, by theme (holy sites, tourist-friendly)

**Tactics:**
- Title and description: keywords (mosques, visit, bucket list, country, tourist)
- End screen: subscribe + link to site
- No sensational thumbnails; keep tone respectful

**Posting:** 1–2 Shorts per month; 1 longer video per quarter if resources allow.

---

## Email (when you have a list)

**Goals:** Re-engage, feature updates, seasonal nudges.

**Content mix:**
- Welcome: “Here’s how to get started — explore, save, and track”
- Monthly or quarterly: “New mosques and features,” “Ramadan: add one mosque to your list”
- Light personalization: “You have X on your list — here’s one more in [country]” (if data exists)

**Tactics:**
- Subject lines from [social-announcements.md](./social-announcements.md)
- One primary CTA per email; mobile-friendly
- Unsubscribe and privacy in footer; don’t over-email (max 2–4/month)

---

## Summary

| Channel   | Primary audience        | Posting frequency | Best content type        |
|-----------|-------------------------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| Instagram | Muslims, travelers      | 3–5/week          | Carousel, Reel            |
| Twitter/X | Tech, travel, Islamic   | 5–7/week          | Thread, single link       |
| TikTok    | Younger, discovery      | 2–4/week          | 15–30s video              |
| Facebook  | Community, families     | 2–3/week          | Post + link               |
| LinkedIn  | Partners, press         | 1–2/month         | Announcement, article     |
| Pinterest | Evergreen searchers     | 5–10 pins/week    | Pin + board                |
| YouTube   | How-to searchers        | 1–2/month         | Shorts, guide              |

Adjust frequency and mix based on capacity and what drives the most site visits or list adds.

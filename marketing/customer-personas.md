# Customer Personas — MosqueList

Use these personas to shape campaigns, ad copy, and content. For deeper audience notes see `../docs/personas.md`.

---

## Persona 1: Ummah-focused Muslim

**Who:** Practicing Muslims (any age, global) who care about holy sites, Umrah/Hajj, or a “mosque bucket list.”

**Goals:**
- Visit the three holiest sites (Mecca, Medina, Jerusalem) and other significant mosques
- Get accurate Islamic context (names, significance, respect for scripture)
- Discover mosques by holiness, country, capacity; save and track visits

**Pain points:**
- Scattered info; hard to compare mosques or plan a spiritual journey in one place
- Worry about accuracy and respectful representation of sacred sites

**How we speak to them:**
- Reverent, accurate language (e.g. “Masjid al-Haram,” “Prophet Muhammad ﷺ”)
- Highlight “Holy Site,” significance, women’s prayer area
- Messaging: “Your journey to the sacred places,” “Track the mosques you’ve visited and plan your next destination”

**Channels:** Islamic and community forums, Ramadan/Hajj-season content, word of mouth, search (sacred mosques, holiest sites).

---

## Persona 2: Muslim traveler & diaspora

**Who:** Muslims living or traveling abroad; want to find a mosque in a new city or plan trips around famous mosques.

**Goals:**
- Filter by country/city; find “biggest mosque in [country],” tourist-friendly options
- Women’s prayer space, visitor info, dress code
- Save favorites and mark “visited”; share lists with family

**Pain points:**
- Generic travel sites don’t surface mosque-specific details (women’s area, tourist access)
- No single place to plan “mosque-focused” trips

**How we speak to them:**
- Practical and welcoming; strong filters (country, tourist-friendly, women’s area)
- Content: “Best mosques to visit in [region],” visitor guides, map view
- Messaging: “Find the world’s most magnificent mosques—filter by country and visitor access”

**Channels:** Travel and lifestyle (Muslim travel), Instagram/TikTok (visual discovery), SEO (e.g. “mosques in Istanbul,” “tourist-friendly mosques”).

---

## Persona 3: Cultural traveler (non-Muslim)

**Who:** Tourists who visit mosques as cultural and architectural landmarks (Blue Mosque, Sheikh Zayed, Hassan II).

**Goals:**
- “Can I visit?” — opening hours, dress code, photography rules
- Brief, neutral context (history, architecture) without religious depth
- Inspiration for itinerary (e.g. “top mosques to see in Turkey”)

**Pain points:**
- Unclear if non-Muslims can enter; fear of offending; lack of concise visitor tips

**How we speak to them:**
- Neutral, informative tone; “Tourist Friendly” badge and visitor tips
- Content: dress code, best times to visit, etiquette; avoid proselytizing
- Messaging: “Discover iconic mosques open to visitors—with practical tips for your visit”

**Channels:** General travel SEO, Pinterest, travel bloggers, “things to do in [city]” content.

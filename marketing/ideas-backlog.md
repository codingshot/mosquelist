# Marketing Ideas Backlog — MosqueList

Running list of campaign ideas, content angles, and experiments. **Internal only.** Add new ideas here; move to “Done” or “Scheduled” when executed. Prioritize by impact and effort.

---

## Content & campaign ideas

- [ ] **“5 mosques in [country]” series** — One post/carousel per country (Turkey, UAE, Malaysia, Pakistan, etc.); link to explore filtered by country.
- [ ] **“Before you visit” short guides** — One-pagers or Reels for top 10 tourist-friendly mosques (dress code, hours, one fun fact).
- [ ] **Timeline takeover** — “On this day in Islamic history: [mosque] completed in [year]” using timeline data.
- [ ] **Bucket list bingo** — Printable or shareable “mosque bucket list bingo” card (9–12 famous mosques); share your progress.
- [ ] **User story series** — “I used MosqueList to plan my trip to [region]” (with permission); 3–5 short testimonials.
- [ ] **Largest mosque per continent** — One visual or carousel: Africa, Asia, Europe, etc., with one mosque each and link to explore.
- [ ] **Ramadan countdown** — “X days to Ramadan — add one mosque to your list today” (daily or weekly prompt).
- [ ] **Eid around the world** — “Mosques where Eid is celebrated” (image + caption); link to map/list.
- [ ] **Hadith + mosque** — Pair a short, well-known hadith about mosques with a beautiful mosque image; no interpretation, just reflection.
- [ ] **“First time visiting a mosque?”** — Single page or Reel: dress code, etiquette, what to expect; position MosqueList as the discovery layer.
- [ ] **Year in review** — “In 20XX we added [N] mosques, [N] countries; here are the most saved.” (When you have basic analytics.)
- [ ] **Collaboration with travel creator** — One Muslim travel or cultural-travel creator: “How I use MosqueList to plan trips.”

## Partnership & distribution ideas

- [ ] **Islamic student associations (MSA / ISOC)** — Offer “plan your mosque bucket list” workshop or link in newsletter.
- [ ] **Travel bloggers** — “Best mosques to visit in [city]” — suggest they cite MosqueList as the tool to explore further.
- [ ] **Hajj/Umrah prep content** — We don’t sell packages; partner with a reputable prep site to “discover the three holiest sites” via MosqueList.
- [ ] **Mosque-run social accounts** — Feature one mosque per month; tag them (with permission); they may reshare.
- [ ] **University Islamic studies / architecture** — “Resource for exploring mosque history and geography.”
- [ ] **App directories** — Submit to “best Muslim apps,” “travel planning tools,” “bucket list apps” roundups.

---

## Product-led growth ideas

- [ ] **Shareable bucket list** — “Share my list” generates a clean link or image (privacy-safe); viral potential.
- [ ] **“Add to my list” from mosque page** — Strong CTA; consider “Copy link to this mosque” for WhatsApp/email.
- [ ] **Email or in-app “You have X mosques on your list — here’s one to explore”** — Light re-engagement (if you add email).
- [ ] **Embed widget** — “Add our mosque explorer to your site” (iframe or link) for Islamic/travel sites.
- [ ] **QR at mosques** — “Scan to learn more about this mosque” (if mosque admin agrees); link to MosqueList mosque page.

---

## Experiments (test & learn)

- [ ] **A/B headline on landing** — “Your journey to the sacred places” vs “Discover 80+ mosques and plan your visits.”
- [ ] **Carousel vs single image** — Same content (e.g. “5 mosques in Turkey”): carousel vs one image with text overlay; measure engagement.
- [ ] **Hashtag set A vs B** — Two posts, same creative; different hashtags; compare reach/saves.
- [ ] **Short video (15s) vs static** — Same message; Reel/Short vs image; compare completion and link clicks.
- [ ] **Paid test** — Small budget ($50–100) on Meta or Google during Ramadan; “Discover and plan your mosque visits” → landing page; measure sign-ups or list adds if trackable.

# Pitch and Messaging — MosqueList

Core pitch, value propositions, and key messages for internal use (sales, partners, press, ads). **Do not publish this document publicly.**

---

## Elevator pitch (30 seconds)

**MosqueList is a free web app that helps people discover and plan visits to the world's most significant mosques.**

You can explore 199+ mosques in 50+ countries—from the three holiest sites (Mecca, Medina, Jerusalem) to iconic landmarks—filter by country, capacity, and visitor access, build a personal mosque bucket list, and see everything on an interactive map. Our fact-checked **Blog** covers visitor etiquette, Islamic architecture, funding, and spiritual travel; our **Islamic History** timeline and **Glossary** add context. MosqueList is for Muslims planning a spiritual journey, travelers looking for tourist-friendly mosques, and anyone curious about Islamic heritage. We focus on accuracy, sources, and respect for sacred sites.

## What we are not (positioning)

- We are **not** a Hajj/Umrah operator or visa service.
- We are **not** a prayer-times or Qibla app (we link out where useful).
- We are **not** a scholarly or fatwa source—we describe mosques and history, not religious rulings.
- We **are** a discovery and planning tool: explore, save, plan, and learn—with a map, blog, and timeline.

---

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Why not just use Google?" | MosqueList brings capacity, significance, tourist-friendly, women's area, bucket-list tracking, and 35+ fact-checked articles in one place—curated for mosque discovery and trip planning, with sources. |
| "Is it only for Muslims?" | No. It's for anyone who wants to discover and visit significant mosques—Muslims planning a spiritual journey and travelers interested in culture and architecture. Our visitor guides are written for both. |
| "How do you make money?" | Currently free; no ads on sacred content. Future options might include optional supporter/donation or non-intrusive sponsorship—always respectful of the subject matter. |
| "How accurate is the data?" | We use a fact-check process, link to Wikipedia and official sources on mosque pages, and correct errors when reported. We don't claim scholarly authority—we describe mosques and context with traceable references. |

# Pitch and Messaging — MosqueList

Core pitch, value propositions, and key messages for internal use (sales, partners, press, ads). **Do not publish this document publicly.**

---

## Elevator pitch (30 seconds)

**MosqueList is a free web app that helps people discover and plan visits to the world’s most significant mosques.**

You can explore 80+ mosques—from the three holiest sites (Mecca, Medina, Jerusalem) to iconic mosques in 40+ countries—filter by country, capacity, and visitor access, build a personal mosque bucket list, and see everything on a map. It’s for Muslims planning a spiritual journey, travelers looking for tourist-friendly mosques, and anyone curious about Islamic heritage and architecture. We focus on accuracy and respect for sacred sites.

## Value propositions by audience

| Audience | Value prop |
|----------|------------|
| **Ummah-focused Muslims** | One place to discover sacred and significant mosques, save favorites, and track your spiritual journey—with accurate names and significance. |
| **Muslim travelers** | Find mosques by country and city; see tourist-friendly options and women’s prayer areas; plan trips and track where you’ve prayed. |
| **Cultural travelers** | Discover which mosques you can visit, with practical tips (dress code, etiquette) and brief history—no proselytizing. |
| **Partners / press** | A clear, respectful product narrative: discovery + planning + bucket list for significant mosques worldwide. |

## Key messages (use in copy and talking points)

1. **Discovery:** “Discover the world’s most significant mosques in one place—holy sites, architectural landmarks, by country and region.”
2. **Planning:** “Plan your visits: filter by country, capacity, tourist-friendly, and women’s prayer area; see mosques on a map.”
3. **Bucket list:** “Build your mosque bucket list and track the places you’ve prayed—your journey, your list.”
4. **Trust:** “We focus on accuracy and respect for sacred sites—correct names, significance, and visitor information.”
5. **Inclusive:** “For Muslims planning a spiritual journey and for travelers curious about Islamic heritage—everyone welcome.”

---

## What we are not (positioning)

- We are **not** a Hajj/Umrah operator or visa service.
- We are **not** a prayer-times or Qibla app (we link out where useful).
- We are **not** a scholarly or fatwa source—we describe mosques and history, not religious rulings.
- We **are** a discovery and planning tool: explore, save, and plan visits to significant mosques.

---

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| “Why not just use Google?” | MosqueList brings capacity, significance, tourist-friendly, women’s area, and bucket-list tracking in one place—curated for mosque discovery and trip planning. |
| “Is it only for Muslims?” | No. It’s for anyone who wants to discover and visit significant mosques—Muslims planning a spiritual journey and travelers interested in culture and architecture. |
| “How do you make money?” | Currently free; no ads on sacred content. Future options might include optional supporter/donation or non-intrusive sponsorship—always respectful of the subject matter. |
| “How accurate is the data?” | We use authoritative sources and a fact-check process; we correct errors when reported and don’t claim scholarly authority—we describe mosques and context. |

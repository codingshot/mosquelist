# Partnership & Outreach — MosqueList

Ideas for partners, collaborators, and outreach templates. **Internal only.** Customize before sending; keep tone professional and value-first.

---

## Partner categories

| Category | Who | Value we offer | What we ask |
|----------|-----|----------------|-------------|
| **Islamic student associations** | MSA, ISOC, university groups | Free resource for “mosque bucket list,” trip planning, discovery | Link in newsletter, one social post, or short workshop mention |
| **Muslim travel creators** | Bloggers, YouTubers, Instagram | Accurate mosque list, map, filters; content angle (“how I use MosqueList”) | Honest review, one video or post, link in bio/description |
| **Travel bloggers (general)** | Culture/travel focus | Visitor-friendly mosque list, dress code/etiquette tips, “things to do” angle | “Best mosques to visit in [city]” — cite MosqueList as the tool to explore |
| **Hajj/Umrah prep (non-operator)** | Info sites, prep guides | We don’t sell packages; we offer “discover the three holiest sites” and context | Link to MosqueList for discovery; we link back to them for visas/packages if appropriate |
| **Mosques & Islamic centers** | Mosque social accounts, boards | Feature their mosque; accurate listing; “learn more” link | Reshare or tag (with permission); add their mosque if not yet listed |
| **App / product directories** | “Best Muslim apps,” “Travel tools” | Clear description, free tool, no ads on sacred content | Inclusion in roundup; we provide blurb and link |
| **Press & media** | Travel, tech, culture desks | Story: “Free app to discover and plan mosque visits”; founder quote | Coverage; we provide assets and quote |

## What to send with outreach

- **Link:** [mosquelist.com] (or current URL)
- **One-liner:** From [pitch-and-messaging.md](./pitch-and-messaging.md)
- **Assets (if asked):** Logo, app screenshot, 1–2 mosque images (rights-cleared)
- **Don’t:** Attach large files unsolicited; don’t over-follow (one follow-up max unless they reply)

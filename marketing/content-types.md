# Content Types to Post — MosqueList

What to create and where to use it. Align with [customer-personas.md](./customer-personas.md) and [voice-and-tone.md](./voice-and-tone.md).

---

## 1. Evergreen educational

**Purpose:** Build authority and SEO; answer “what is,” “where is,” “biggest,” “holiest.”

**Examples:**
- “The three holiest mosques in Islam” (Mecca, Medina, Jerusalem)
- “Largest mosques in the world by capacity”
- “Biggest mosque in [country]” (Pakistan, Indonesia, Turkey, UAE, etc.)
- “Visitor tips: dress code and etiquette at mosques”
- Short “History of [Mosque Name]” or timeline snippets

**Formats:** Blog or in-app guides, carousel posts, short videos (60–90 sec).

**Post where:** Website blog/guides, Instagram carousels, LinkedIn articles, Pinterest, YouTube Shorts.

---

## 2. Inspirational & bucket-list

**Purpose:** Aspiration; “places to pray,” “mosques to visit before…”

**Examples:**
- “5 mosques to add to your bucket list”
- “From Istanbul to Jakarta: a journey through iconic mosques”
- User journey: “I checked off Masjid an-Nabawi — here’s how I planned it”
- Quote + mosque image: “The best of places are the mosques…” (hadith)

**Formats:** Image + caption, carousel (one mosque per slide), short video montage.

**Post where:** Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, Facebook; Stories.

---

## 3. Practical & how-to

**Purpose:** Solve “how do I…” and “can I visit?”

**Examples:**
- “How to plan a mosque-focused trip”
- “What to wear when visiting a mosque”
- “Using MosqueList: explore, save, and track your visits”
- “Best time to visit [mosque/city]”

**Formats:** How-to blog, Reel/Short, thread (Twitter), infographic.

**Post where:** Blog, Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest.

---

## 4. Product & feature updates

**Purpose:** Announce new features and remind people what MosqueList does.

**Examples:**
- “We added a map — explore mosques by country and region”
- “New: related mosques on every mosque page”
- “Curated lists: Holy Sites, Biggest Mosques, by country”
- “You can now build your mosque bucket list and mark visits”

**Formats:** Short announcement post, 15–30 sec product clip, email subject line.

**Post where:** All social; newsletter if you have one; in-app banner or release notes.

---

## 5. Seasonal & moment-based

**Purpose:** Tie into Ramadan, Hajj, Eid, travel season, Islamic calendar.

**Examples:**
- Ramadan: “Plan your spiritual journey — explore and save mosques”
- Pre-Hajj/Umrah: “The three holiest sites and how to prepare”
- Eid: “Mosques around the world celebrating Eid”
- New year: “New year, new list: 5 mosques to visit this year”

**Formats:** Themed image or short video, limited-time CTA.

**Post where:** All social; consider light paid boost during Ramadan/Hajj.

---

## 6. Community & UGC (future)

**Purpose:** Build trust and relatability (when you have permission).

**Examples:**
- “Screenshot of your bucket list” (with consent)
- “Visited the Blue Mosque — here’s my list so far”
- Testimonial or short quote from a user

**Formats:** Repost (with credit), quote graphic, short testimonial video.

**Post where:** Instagram, Twitter/X, Facebook; use only with clear permission.

---

## Content calendar priorities

| Priority | Content type              | Frequency (suggestion) |
|----------|---------------------------|-------------------------|
| High     | Evergreen educational     | 1–2/week (blog + 1 social) |
| High     | Inspirational / bucket list| 2–3/week (social)       |
| Medium   | Practical how-to          | 1/week                  |
| Medium   | Product/feature updates   | On release              |
| Medium   | Seasonal                  | Around Ramadan, Hajj, Eid |
| Lower    | Community/UGC             | As available (with permission) |

Adjust by channel: e.g. more Reels/Shorts on Instagram/TikTok, more threads/articles on Twitter/LinkedIn.

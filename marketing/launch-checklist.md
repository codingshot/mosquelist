# Launch & Campaign Checklist — MosqueList

Use before a product launch, feature release, or campaign. **Internal only.** Tick as you go; adapt to scope.

---

## Pre-launch (1–2 weeks before)

### Product & content
- [ ] Feature or product is tested and stable (no critical bugs on main flows).
- [ ] Landing page / hero copy is final (or A/B variant chosen).
- [ ] Key pages have correct meta title and description (SEO).
- [ ] Any new mosque data or lists are fact-checked and live.
- [ ] Link to be promoted is correct (e.g. mosquelist.com or /explore).

### Assets
- [ ] At least one share image (1200x630 or similar for social).
- [ ] Short video or Reel (15–30 sec) if doing video launch.
- [ ] One-liner and 2–3 key messages written (from [pitch-and-messaging.md](./pitch-and-messaging.md)).
- [ ] Press/partner one-liner ready (from [social-announcements.md](./social-announcements.md)).

### Channels
- [ ] Decide which channels to use (Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, etc.).
- [ ] Draft posts per channel (use [social-announcements.md](./social-announcements.md)).
- [ ] Schedule or set reminder for launch day posts.
- [ ] If email: draft subject line and body; list ready.

---

## Launch day

### Publish
- [ ] Deploy or flip feature if applicable.
- [ ] Publish social posts (stagger by 1–2 hours if multiple channels).
- [ ] Send email if applicable.
- [ ] Post in any agreed partner or community channels (with permission).

### Monitor
- [ ] Check link works from each post.
- [ ] Watch for replies or DMs; respond in line with [voice-and-tone.md](./voice-and-tone.md).
- [ ] Note any errors (e.g. 404, broken image) and fix quickly.

---

## Post-launch (within 1 week)

- [ ] Note what performed best (engagement, clicks, sign-ups if trackable).
- [ ] Thank anyone who shared or partnered.
- [ ] Update [social-announcements.md](./social-announcements.md) or [pitch-and-messaging.md](./pitch-and-messaging.md) if messaging evolved.
- [ ] Add “what we’d do differently” to [ideas-backlog.md](./ideas-backlog.md) for next time.

---

## Seasonal campaign (e.g. Ramadan)

- [ ] Confirm dates (Islamic calendar).
- [ ] Theme and angle decided (from [seasonal-calendar.md](./seasonal-calendar.md)).
- [ ] Copy and creative are reverent and aligned with [voice-and-tone.md](./voice-and-tone.md).
- [ ] No promise of religious services we don’t provide (e.g. Hajj booking).
- [ ] If paid: audience targeting and budget set; landing page ready.
- [ ] Launch and post-launch steps above applied.

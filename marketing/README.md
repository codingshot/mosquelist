## Quick reference

- **Product:** MosqueList — discover, explore, and plan visits to the world's most significant mosques; build a mosque bucket list.
- **Audience:** Muslims (ummah-focused), Muslim travelers & diaspora, cultural travelers (non-Muslim).
- **Tone:** Reverent, accurate, welcoming; avoid casual or sensational.
- **Do not:** Publish or link this `/marketing` folder on the live site; treat as internal knowledge base only.

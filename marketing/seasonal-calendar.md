# Seasonal & Moment Calendar — MosqueList

Key dates and moments to plan content, campaigns, and partnerships. **Internal only.** Use with [content-types.md](./content-types.md) and [ideas-backlog.md](./ideas-backlog.md).

---

## Islamic calendar (approximate — verify each year)

| Period / date       | Content & campaign ideas |
|---------------------|---------------------------|
| **Ramadan**         | “Plan your spiritual journey”; “Add one mosque to your list this Ramadan”; evergreen mosque discovery; light paid boost if budget. |
| **Eid al-Fitr**     | “Eid Mubarak — mosques around the world”; celebratory image + link to explore; avoid commercial tone. |
| **Hajj season**     | “The three holiest sites” content; visitor prep (we don’t sell Hajj); link to Mecca, Medina, Al-Aqsa on MosqueList. |
| **Eid al-Adha**     | Similar to Eid al-Fitr; “Places to pray” angle; share map or holy sites list. |
| **Islamic New Year (Muharram)** | “A new year, a new list”; gentle CTA to explore and save mosques. |
| **Mawlid (Prophet’s birthday)** | Historical mosques (e.g. Al-Masjid an-Nabawi); reverent tone; no parties or commercial framing. |
| **Laylat al-Qadr**  | Optional: “While you prepare, discover the sacred places”; low-key, no hype. |

## Do’s and don’ts for seasonal content

- **Do:** Verify Islamic calendar dates each year (lunar calendar shifts).
- **Do:** Keep sacred moments reverent; no salesy or hype tone.
- **Do:** Plan 2–4 weeks ahead so assets and copy are ready.
- **Don’t:** Use sacred events purely for engagement tricks.
- **Don’t:** Promise Hajj/Umrah services or visas; we only help discover and plan.

Reference [voice-and-tone.md](./voice-and-tone.md) for tone during religious moments.

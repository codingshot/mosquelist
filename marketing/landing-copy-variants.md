# Landing Copy Variants — MosqueList

Headline, subhead, and CTA variants for landing page, ads, and A/B tests. **Internal only.** Use with [voice-and-tone.md](./voice-and-tone.md); avoid hype or ALL CAPS.

---

## Hero / above the fold

**Headline options (pick one per variant):**

- Your journey to the sacred places begins here.
- Discover the world’s most magnificent mosques.
- Explore, save, and plan your mosque bucket list.
- From the three holiest sites to iconic mosques worldwide.
- One place to discover 80+ mosques — and plan your visits.

- Explore 80+ mosques in 40+ countries. Filter by country, save favorites, and build your personal prayer bucket list.

## A/B test ideas

| Test | Variant A | Variant B | Metric |
|------|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Hero headline | Your journey to the sacred places | Discover 80+ mosques and plan your visits | Clicks to Explore / time on page |
| Primary CTA | Start your journey | Explore mosques | Click-through rate |
| Subhead length | Short (one sentence) | Long (two sentences) | Scroll depth / sign-up (if any) |
| Value prop order | Discovery first | Bucket list first | Scroll to list section |

Run one test at a time; wait for statistical significance before switching.

---

## Don’t use

- “OMG,” “You won’t believe,” “Mind-blowing”
- ALL CAPS for emphasis
- Promises we don’t deliver (e.g. “Book Hajj here”)
- Irreverent or casual language about holy sites

Keep all variants aligned with [voice-and-tone.md](./voice-and-tone.md).

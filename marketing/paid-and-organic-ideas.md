# Paid & Organic Growth Ideas — MosqueList

Ideas for organic growth and lightweight paid efforts. **Internal only.** Prioritize organic first; use paid to test or amplify when budget allows.

---

## Organic growth

### SEO & discoverability
- [ ] **Target keywords:** “mosques to visit,” “biggest mosque in [country],” “holy sites Islam,” “tourist friendly mosques [city],” “mosque bucket list.”
- [ ] **On-site:** Unique meta title/description per page (explore, mosque pages, lists, guides); clean URLs; internal links (e.g. related mosques, lists).
- [ ] **Content:** Expand [Travel Guide](/guides/travel) and [Visitor Tips](/guides/visitor-tips); add “Largest mosques in the world,” “Three holiest sites” as dedicated pages or sections if not already.
- [ ] **Structured data:** Ensure mosque and list schema (JSON-LD) is correct for rich results.
- [ ] **Backlinks:** Partner and outreach (see [partnership-outreach.md](./partnership-outreach.md)); get cited in “best mosques to visit” and “Muslim travel resources” roundups.

### Social & community
- [ ] **Consistent posting:** Use [channel-playbook.md](./channel-playbook.md); focus on 2–3 channels where audience is (e.g. Instagram, Twitter).
- [ ] **Engagement:** Reply to comments and DMs; join conversations about mosque travel, bucket lists, Islamic heritage (no spam).
- [ ] **Shareable moments:** “Share my bucket list” or “I added X to my list” (if you build share feature); encourage tagging #MosqueList.
- [ ] **UGC (with permission):** Reshare user stories or screenshots; always credit and get consent.

### Product-led
- [ ] **Clear CTAs:** “Explore mosques,” “Add to my list,” “See on map” — above the fold and in key flows.
- [ ] **Low friction:** No sign-up required to explore; optional save/list so first visit is useful.
- [ ] **Re-engagement:** If you add email or push: “You have X on your list — here’s one more in [country]” (light, not spammy).
- [ ] **Referral (future):** “Share MosqueList with a friend” with simple link or pre-filled message.

## Paid (when budget exists)

### Goals for paid
- **Awareness:** “Discover and plan mosque visits” to Muslims and travel-interested users.
- **Traffic:** Drive clicks to site; measure visits and (if trackable) list adds or explore usage.
- **Test:** Small budget to see which creative, audience, or platform works before scaling.

### Meta (Facebook / Instagram)
- **Audience:** Interest in Islam, Islamic culture, travel, bucket list; geography: countries with large Muslim or travel audience.
- **Creative:** Image or short video (15s); headline from [landing-copy-variants.md](./landing-copy-variants.md); CTA “Learn more” or “Visit site.”
- **Budget:** Start with $50–100 over 1–2 weeks; optimize for link clicks or landing page views.
- **Best moments:** Ramadan, Hajj prep, New Year (“start your mosque bucket list”).
- **Don’t:** Use sacred imagery in a sensational way; keep tone from [voice-and-tone.md](./voice-and-tone.md).

### Google (Search & Display)
- **Search:** Keywords: “mosques to visit,” “biggest mosque,” “mosque bucket list,” “tourist friendly mosques.” Landing page: home or /explore.
- **Display:** Remarketing only if you have enough visitors (e.g. “You visited MosqueList — explore by country”).
- **Budget:** Start small ($30–50); focus on search if budget is very limited.

### TikTok / YouTube (optional)
- **TikTok:** Promote a Reel-style ad (“Where do you want to pray next?”); audience: interest in travel, culture, Islam.
- **YouTube:** Skippable in-stream or Shorts; same creative as organic Shorts; CTA to site.
- **Budget:** Test with $30–50; compare cost per click to Meta.

### Measurement (minimal)
- **Must:** Link clicks, landing page visits (e.g. UTM: ?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=ramadan2025).
- **Nice:** Time on site, scroll depth, or “Explore” button clicks (if you add a simple event).
- **Don’t:** Track religious or sensitive data; keep privacy-first.

## Priority order (no / low budget)

1. **SEO + content** — Evergreen; use [content-types.md](./content-types.md).
2. **Organic social** — 2–3 channels; use [channel-playbook.md](./channel-playbook.md) and [social-announcements.md](./social-announcements.md).
3. **Partnerships** — Use [partnership-outreach.md](./partnership-outreach.md); free distribution.
4. **Light paid test** — Only when you have $50–100 to learn; Ramadan or launch.

---

## What to avoid

- Paying for followers or engagement (fake or low intent).
- Aggressive retargeting or creepy personalization.
- Ads that sensationalize sacred sites or use religion purely for clicks.
- Promising Hajj/Umrah, visas, or prayer times if we don’t offer them.

Keep all paid creative and targeting aligned with [voice-and-tone.md](./voice-and-tone.md) and [pitch-and-messaging.md](./pitch-and-messaging.md).
